Static design prototype for internal review. Not for distribution.

## Pages

| | |
|---|---|
| [Landing](https://thatmattgillespie.github.io/peaceintel-style-review/) | General landing page, with an audience selector under the hero |
| [For Funders & Leadership](https://thatmattgillespie.github.io/peaceintel-style-review/funders.html) | Portfolio decisions, strategic briefings, evidence gaps |

The three audience pages cross-link to each other in the nav and in the hero tab row.

## Notes for reviewers

- The warm direction is locked. The earlier Warm/Clean A/B toggle has been removed.
- Type is DIN 2014 (self-hosted from `Fonts/`), Nanum Myeongjo, and Space Mono.
- The use-case card graphics are static stills built in HTML/CSS from the real app components. They are not screenshots and not interactive.
- **Open question:** the corpus statistics on the landing page and on the three audience pages do not currently agree. One set is stale and needs a decision.

Design tokens live in `DESIGN-TOKENS.md` in the working repo. The HTML here is the source of truth for anything the two disagree on.
